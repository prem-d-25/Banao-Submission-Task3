# Note to the Procurement Lead

**Subject:** Faster shade correction for your printed tiles: our proposal

Dear Special Projects Lead,

Thank you for the detailed walkthrough. Here is our proposal in plain terms.

## What we understood
When a tile design is run again, the shade often no longer matches the master tile. Your team then fixes the print file by eye and tests 6 or 7 versions. This takes 6 to 7 hours per round, and meanwhile thousands of square metres can come out in the wrong shade.

You already measure how far off a tile is. What is missing is a clear answer to: **"how much should we change the print file?"**

## What we propose
A simple tool that takes your colour measurements of the master tile and the current tile and **suggests the exact change to the print file**. Your QC team reviews and approves every suggestion. One test tile is then fired to confirm before the batch runs.

- It uses the measuring device you already own. **No cameras or new hardware.**
- It runs on a normal laptop at the plant. **No connection to your printer or factory network.**
- It starts with **one plant (India) and 6 to 8 tile designs**. If it works, you can expand.

## What to expect, honestly
We expect a correction to drop from about **6.5 hours to about 2 to 3 hours**, with fewer wasted tiles. Because a test tile still has to be fired in the kiln, we do **not** promise "5 to 6 minutes" in this first phase. A later, optional phase could aim for minutes, and we would only recommend it if the data shows it is realistic.

## What we need from you
1. **Your QC and design teams on our next call.** They use this daily, and we want to build what they need.
2. A visit to the India plant.
3. Master tiles, a few sample variation tiles, and any past correction records.
4. Agreement on what counts as a "match" and which designs to start with.
5. Access to the kiln for about 13 test tiles per design group, and a data export from your measuring device.

## What it costs
- **Pilot price: about ₹40,00,000 (around US$47,000)**, covering about 11 weeks of work.
- **Payment tied to results:** 30% at kickoff, 40% when you see the first result, 30% at the final review.
- After the pilot: about ₹1,00,000 per month for support, and about ₹60,000 to prepare each new group of designs.
- Not included: kiln time, test tiles, and a laptop for the plant.

These figures rest on assumptions we will confirm with your team.

## What it could be worth
If the tool cuts wasted tiles by half, our estimate is that the pilot pays for itself in roughly **3 months** after going live, and in about 11 months in a cautious case. The real number depends on your batch changes and cost per square metre, which we would like to confirm with you.

## When you will see results
- **Week 2:** your teams are involved; designs and match standard agreed.
- **Week 5:** real numbers on today's rounds, hours and wasted tiles.
- **Week 7: first result** showing whether the tool finds the right fix on past cases.
- **Week 11:** joint decision to expand, adjust or stop. If it does not work, we will say so early.

We would be glad to arrange a short call this week, ideally with your QC and design leads.
